Privacy Policy

DeFaria Clips · Last updated August 19, 2026

DeFaria Clips ("the App", "we", "us") is an internal content-operations tool operated by DeFaria NYC LLC. It helps our team edit short-form video clips and publish them to social media accounts that we own or are authorized to manage. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

Who uses this App

The App is used only by DeFaria NYC LLC and the account owners who have explicitly connected their own accounts to it. It is not offered to the general public and does not collect data from third-party consumers.

What data we access

  • Account authorization tokens. When an account owner connects their TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube account, the platform provides an access token that lets the App publish content on that account's behalf. We store these tokens securely and never share them.
  • Basic account identifiers. The connected account's username and account ID, used to route each post to the correct account.
  • Publishing results. After a post is published, the App reads the resulting post URL and its public performance metrics (views, likes, comments) to produce internal performance reports.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect or store passwords. Connection happens through each platform's official authorization flow.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not access private messages, contacts, or any data beyond what is needed to publish and measure our own posts.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling of any individual.

How data is stored

Authorization tokens and account identifiers are stored locally on hardware controlled by DeFaria NYC LLC, in access-restricted files. They are never transmitted to any party other than the originating platform's own API.

How to revoke access

An account owner can disconnect the App at any time from their platform's connected-apps settings (TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube security settings). Revoking access immediately stops the App from publishing on that account, and any stored token for it is deleted.

Data retention

Tokens are retained only while an account remains connected. Public performance metrics used for reporting are retained for internal analysis and may be deleted on request.
